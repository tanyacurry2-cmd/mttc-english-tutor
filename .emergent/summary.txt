<product_requirements>
The MTTC English Tutor is an iOS app for MTTC English 002 exam preparation using Expo/React Native. Its core purpose is to provide comprehensive study tools. Features include:
- A 20-question diagnostic assessment with timer, session history, and readiness score.
- Study modes: Learn for flashcards, Drill for MCQs.
- Practice screen for subarea performance.
- Progress heatmap for tracking.
- Firebase Auth (placeholders present) and Apple Sign-In (planned).
- In-App Purchases (IAP) for premium features.
Content, initially local JSON, is now migrated to a FastAPI backend with MongoDB for dynamic updates. The app prioritizes smart content for unseen/least-seen questions, features confetti for mastery/perfect scores, and includes a mastery tracking system with reinstate options. The home screen provides quick access to unmastered content. Recently, an AI-powered Writing Lab for rubric-style feedback on constructed responses was added. The app aims for a professional, calm UI/UX, seamless offline functionality via caching, and is currently being prepared for TestFlight deployment.
</product_requirements>
